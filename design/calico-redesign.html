UX changes

What changes for the people at the counter

Everywhere

  • Light headers with serif titles replace the navy bars.
  • Every tab has an account button (initials) with sign-out, so Reports no longer holds it. Staff get 3 tabs instead of 4.
  • Saving shows a short confirmation toast instead of a blocking alert.
  • Search fields show an icon and a clear button. Empty states say what to do next.

Sell

  • The order matches a real sale: items, then customer, then payment.
  • Quantity uses a −/+ stepper capped at the stock on hand. Discount stays hidden until it's needed.
  • The item picker shows stock levels and blocks out-of-stock items.
  • Payment is one control: Paid in full, Part paid or On credit.
  • The date defaults to today, with a Yesterday shortcut.
  • The total and Save sale stay pinned above the keyboard.

Stock

  • Filter chips show how many items each filter matches.
  • Stock counts and status pills are colour-coded so problems stand out.
  • The restock sheet has +1, +5 and +10 shortcuts and shows the new total.
  • Owners get a floating "New item" button.

Customers & payments

  • The total owed is the headline, and the "owes money" filter is sorted by largest debt.
  • The customer page leads with the balance, with Full and Half quick amounts.
  • Each purchase shows paid against total at a glance.

Expenses & reports

  • This month's spend is the headline, and new expenses are added from a floating button.
  • Expenses open in a bottom sheet with a large amount field.
  • Reports are a clear P&L with a revenue-split bar and a month switcher.
